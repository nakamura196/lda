446 articles from issues 1–10 of *Yanaka, Nezu, Sendagi*, the community magazine founded by Yanesen Kobo in 1984.
They were digitized by the [Local Cultural Resources Digital Archive Project](https://tcha.jp/pts/lcrd/) with the cooperation of Yanesen Kobo and Mayumi Mori.
Images, metadata and annotations are openly available. The records are in Japanese.
